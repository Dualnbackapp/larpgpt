Privacy Policy

Effective 27 August 2026 · LarpGPT (iOS)

LarpGPT collects no personal data. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server. Nothing is transmitted anywhere.

No collection

We do not collect, store or transmit any personal data. In practice, the app never asks you for:

  • a name, email address or phone number
  • an account or a sign-in
  • access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location

Data stored on your device

Your preferences — chosen language, selected worlds, favourites, onboarding progress — are saved only on your iPhone, using standard iOS storage. They never leave the device and we have no access to them. Deleting the app erases them permanently.

No trackers, no advertising

LarpGPT contains no analytics tool, no advertising SDK and no third-party tracker. No activity is measured or profiled. The app does not request tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency), because it tracks nothing.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see or receive your payment information. We only have access to your subscription status as the operating system reports it to the app, on your device. Management and cancellation happen in your Apple account.

Children

The app is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13. Since no data is collected, no child's data can be collected either.

Your rights

The GDPR and the CCPA grant rights of access, correction and deletion over personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing on our side to consult, correct or delete. The local data is yours and is removed by uninstalling the app.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
