Privacy Policy

Effective 5 September 2026 · LarpGPT (iOS)

The prompt library works offline without an account. The app uses the usage and purchase measurement services described below. It does not transmit your photos or clipboard contents.

No account required

Using the app does not require:

  • a name, email address or phone number
  • an account or a sign-in
  • access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location

Data stored on your device

Your preferences — chosen language, selected worlds, favourites, onboarding progress — are saved on your iPhone using standard iOS storage. Uninstalling the app removes that local storage. Measurement events may include language and onboarding steps, as described below; they are not a backup of your favourites or preferences.

Usage measurement, without advertising

The app sends events to TelemetryDeck, such as opening the app, onboarding steps, viewing an offer and the outcome of a purchase attempt. They help us understand usability problems. They may include the app version, language, configured region, days since installation, selected plan and price. A random installation identifier is hashed before transmission, and a session identifier accompanies events. These identifiers are neither your name nor an advertising identifier.

Photos, clipboard contents, contacts, precise location and payment details are not included in these events. The app has no advertising and does not perform cross-app advertising tracking.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your bank card details. Management and cancellation happen in your Apple account.

RevenueCat receives an identifier generated by its SDK and information about transactions and subscriptions to measure sales. Access within the app is determined by StoreKit.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Please contact us if you believe a child has sent us information.

Your rights

You can contact us with a request about access to, correction of or deletion of your data. We will review the request, the data we can identify and the applicable rules. Uninstalling the app removes its local storage; it does not automatically delete records already sent to measurement services or retained by Apple for purchases. The providers' policies linked above explain their practices.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
