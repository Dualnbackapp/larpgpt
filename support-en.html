How to use a prompt

  • Pick a world
  • Tap once to copy the complete prompt
  • Paste it into your image generator (Midjourney, Flux, Sora, DALL·E)

The prompt already contains the subject, the location, the lighting, the lens and the capture settings. There is nothing to fill in.

The app does not generate images

That is deliberate. LarpGPT is a text library: you bring your own generator. The prompts stay in English, the language every image engine reads most accurately.

Restoring a purchase

Open the app, then tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen. Your purchases are tied to your Apple account, so they follow you across every device using the same Apple ID.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → LarpGPT. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period. We have no control over subscriptions: everything is handled by Apple.

Requesting a refund

Refunds are handled exclusively by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot grant one on your behalf.

The free trial

The weekly and yearly subscriptions include a 3-day free trial. Nothing is charged during the trial. To avoid being billed, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

Privacy

The library works offline. The app uses usage and purchase measurement services, described in the privacy policy.
